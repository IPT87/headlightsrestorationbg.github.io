Professional Headlight Restoration
Over time, headlights can become yellowed, faded, and cloudy, reducing the amount of light they project onto the road. This makes night driving more difficult and can affect your safety. Our restoration service brings your headlights back to life, removing oxidation and restoring their original shine, clarity, and brightness.

Our Multi-Stage Process

01. Decontamination

Thorough cleaning to remove built-up road dirt and surface contaminants.

02. Oxidation Removal

Deep sanding to completely strip away the faded, yellowed oxidized layer.

03. Multi-Stage Polish

Refining the optical lens to restore maximum clarity, shine, and a factory-smooth finish.

04. Ceramic Coating

Application of a premium ceramic UV protective layer with a 1-year warranty*.
